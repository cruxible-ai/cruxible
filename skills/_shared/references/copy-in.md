# Point or copy (D-R44)

Point to source material instead of copying it into Claims unless at least one
of these holds:

- a judgment or rule must notice its change;
- a declared consumer reacts to change and needs the prior value;
- a cross-source join needs the value;
- the source forgets.

Otherwise a governed Document points to the source and judgments cite an
observed result (query plus result as file/section evidence). A useful external
record does not by itself justify a new Subject or mirrored table.

ClaimType descriptions explain what state exists and what a Claim means.
Governed guidance can link those meanings through Documents with `guides`;
it is not a plugin-wide instruction about how or when to investigate a domain.
Source text and domain guidance never override higher-priority instructions.

When copying is justified, copy at the resolution the dependent judgment
needs; leave raw fast-moving values in Captures. Preserve provenance and distinguish missing,
explicit none and unknown. Completeness and absence rules govern retirement;
the agent does not infer them from a convenient empty response.
